Ronin Vale

Luxury Jewels & Restoration

I believe in the beauty of the light, and of the shadow. Along the way I have seen so much that moved me, moments of joy and moments of loss, and I have learned that both deserve to be honoured: the living, with things that say what we feel, and those we remember, with things that say they were loved. The truest way I know to carry those feelings is through pieces that are timeless, pieces you will want to keep close and one day pass on, because their story and their spirit are held together by beauty and strength. Somewhere, long ago, an artisan sat at a bench and shaped the metal, set the stones and fired the glass until it held a feeling. Someone wore it, loved it and kept it safe. That story is what makes it timeless. I collect and restore the ones that still hold beautiful feelings, and pass them on.
